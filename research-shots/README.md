# Research shots

Screenshots and contact sheets captured during the research phase. Kept because they are the
visual evidence behind the written findings — not production assets.

Naming:
- `01–04-*` live HMTI site (hero, full home, about/visi-misi, footer)
- `logo-*` official seal, rendered and cropped
- `instagram-*` / `tiktok-*` / `youtube-*` social evidence
- `polinema-*` / `jti-*` parent-institution evidence
- `*-montage.png` contact sheets assembled for colour/style analysis
