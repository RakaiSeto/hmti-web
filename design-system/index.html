A design system for HMTI Polinema — Himpunan Mahasiswa Teknologi Informasi, Jurusan Teknologi Informasi, Politeknik Negeri Malang. Built from measured evidence, not invented taste: every colour, proportion and rule below traces back to the official seal, the production website, or a national regulation.

01

Three identity layers

HMTI does not have one visual identity — it has three stacked, and only the first is governed. This is the single most important structural fact about the brand, and it decides the scope of everything below.

A

Organisational brand Stable

The seal — yellow badge, royal-blue gear, HMTI wordmark — plus #FFE600. Consistent across every channel ever sampled. This is the brand this system governs.

B

Cabinet campaign Ungoverned

Rebuilt every year. Kabinet Adhigana (2026/2027) runs its Instagram on a maroon/oxblood ground — #5C1A1C–#805954 — with a white→gold script-over-condensed-caps headline lockup. It shares nothing with layer A.

C

Event sub-brands Ungoverned

TI-COMPSTREAM · Seminar Nasional · Malam Keakraban · Study Excursie · Dies Natalis · Expo Kelembagaan. Each has ad-hoc artwork and no templates.

i
A system that only serves layer A will be ignored. The visible output of this organisation is overwhelmingly layers B and C. Either scope the system to A and hand B/C a constrained template set, or build a theming mechanism so a cabinet can re-skin without leaving the system.

Buttons

Voice & tone

Register

Indonesian, formal-institutional but warm. The organisation describes itself as "wadah dan aspirasi serta pelayanan bagi Mahasiswa" — a container and a service, not a club.

Address

The house greeting in captions is "Halo, Sobat Informatics!". Use it for student-facing copy, and the formal register for anything institutional.

Display convention

Uppercase, often spelling out the full legal name. Sentence case for section headings.

Slogans in active use

#TI_FAST #TI_BRAVO #SatuKolaborasiSeribuKontribusi #PROUDTOBEINFORMATICS #HMTI12Polinema
#TI_FAST #TI_BRAVO is declared the company slogan in the LinkedIn JSON-LD and printed on the YouTube banner. #SatuKolaborasiSeribuKontribusi is the Adhigana cabinet slogan. Keep both — they serve different scopes.

Per-intake nicknames

Rasendriya'22 · Arshaknife'24 · Dharmasena'26. Expect a new one every year; the system must not hard-code them.

Open decisions

Ordered by how much each blocks the build. Nothing below is settled by research alone — each needs a call from the organisation.

#DecisionWhy it blocksOur recommendation
1Scope — does the system cover only the org brand, or also the per-cabinet and per-event layers? Decides whether we build a theming mechanism or a fixed palette. Everything downstream depends on it. Serve the org brand, and give cabinets a constrained theme slot (one accent hue + shared template furniture) so they re-skin without leaving the system.
2Canonical blue — #041587? The blue is the biggest unused asset. Every export of the same seal disagrees. Redraw the logo, then re-derive. Until then ship #041587 and treat it as provisional.
3Dark mode — ship it or delete the dead tokens? A complete .dark set is already compiled but never exposed. Ship it. It costs nothing extra now and the accent ramp is already contrast-solved.
4Typeface authority — Montserrat, or Montserrat + Inter? Inter is downloaded on every page load and never used. Montserrat only. Drop Inter unless a numeric/UI use case is identified.
5Radius — token or utility? The declared 0.625rem drives nothing today. Adopt the 7-step scale in §07 and reference the token.
6Vector logo — commission the redraw? No SVG exists. Every variant we need is blocked on it. Yes, and settle the blue at the same time.
7Domain — is hmtipolinema.org becoming canonical? All OG/Twitter metadata already points at it, and it does not resolve. Either point the metadata at hmti.polinema.ac.id or register the domain.
