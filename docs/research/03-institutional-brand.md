# 03 — Institutional Brand: Polinema (and JTI)

Recon of the parent institution whose brand HMTI Polinema must live under.
**Evidence-only.** Every claim carries its source URL. Anything I could not
verify is listed in [UNVERIFIED / NOT FOUND](#unverified--not-found).

Harvested: 2026-09-27 (machine clock). All `www.polinema.ac.id` requests used
`www`, never the apex.

---

## 0. TL;DR for brand decisions

- The **authoritative colour spec is a national regulation**, not a design
  system: *Permenristekdikti No. 20 Tahun 2019 tentang Statuta Polinema*,
  Pasal 3–8, prints exact RGB codes for the lambang, the legacy logo, the
  institutional flag, every departmental flag, and the almamater jacket.
- The regulation's RGB values and the **shipped artwork disagree**. The
  Statuta says the lambang shield field is `0,78,152` (bright blue); every
  PNG Polinema and JTI actually serve renders it as dark navy
  `#2D2766`/`#2D1B6B`. Full table in [§3](#3-exact-colour-table).
- **No institutional tagline or motto exists in any official source.** Not in
  the Statuta, not on the homepage, not on PPID. See §5 and the UNVERIFIED
  section.
- **No published brand guideline exists on a live Polinema domain.** Third-party
  search engines index three "panduan identitas visual" pages; all three are on
  dead or 503 hosts and none are archived. Do not assume one exists.
- **No official SVG of the lambang exists publicly.** Every SVG probe 404s.
  The best available raster is 1140×1138.
- **JTI has no emblem of its own.** Its lockup re-uses the Polinema lambang
  (reversed to white). JTI *does* have a separate abstract "JTI" monogram mark
  used decoratively, plus its own web palette. See §6.
- The **Jurusan Teknologi Informasi flag colour is yellow** per the Statuta —
  the one official hook that makes an HMTI yellow brand defensible.

## 2. Vision / Mission / purpose — official text

### 2a. Statuta wording (binding, national regulation)

> **Pasal 23 — Visi Polinema:** menjadi lembaga pendidikan tinggi vokasi yang
> unggul dalam persaingan global.

> **Pasal 24 — Misi Polinema:**
> a. menyelenggarakan dan mengembangkan pendidikan vokasi yang berkualitas,
> inovatif, dan berdaya saing sesuai kebutuhan industri, lembaga pemerintah,
> dan masyarakat;
> b. menyelenggarakan penelitian terapan dan pengabdian kepada masyarakat yang
> bermanfaat bagi pengembangan ilmu pengetahuan dan teknologi serta
> kesejahteraan masyarakat;
> c. menyelenggarakan sistem pengelolaan pendidikan dengan berdasar pada
> prinsip-prinsip tata pamong yang baik;
> d. menciptakan suasana akademik yang kondusif untuk meningkatkan mutu sumber
> daya manusia dan pembelajaran yang mendorong pola pembelajaran seumur hidup
> dan tumbuhnya jiwa kewirausahaan; dan
> e. mengembangkan kerja sama yang saling menguntungkan dengan berbagai pihak
> baik di dalam maupun di luar negeri pada bidang-bidang yang relevan.

> **Pasal 25 — Tujuan Polinema:** (a) lulusan unggul berdaya saing regional,
> berketuhanan, memegang nilai luhur ke-Indonesia-an; (b) penelitian nasional/
> regional/internasional menuju publikasi, paten, HKI; (c) pengabdian
> masyarakat berbasis teknologi terapan dan jasa; (d) kerja sama produktif
> dalam dan luar negeri; (e) sistem manajemen pendidikan dengan tata kelola
> yang baik.

Source: [Statuta Polinema, Pasal 23–25](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/131546/Permenristekdikti%20Nomor%2020%20Tahun%202019.pdf) (PDF pp. 26–27)

prose source; the Statuta is the normative one.

### 4a. Why the mark looks like this (Statuta Pasal 3 ayat 1–2, verbatim)

> Polinema memiliki lambang berbentuk segi lima berwarna biru, dengan 2 (dua)
> garis tepi berwarna hitam dan putih, yang di dalamnya terdapat: a. roda gigi
> berwarna oranye; b. bola dunia di tengah roda gigi berwarna biru bergradasi
> dengan garis lintang dan garis bujur berwarna putih; c. tugu Kota Malang
> berwarna kuning; d. 3 (tiga) lembar buku berwarna putih; dan e. tulisan
> POLITEKNIK NEGERI MALANG berwarna putih dengan jenis huruf Arial, membentuk
> setengah lingkaran pada bagian atas lambang.
>
> Makna: a. segi 5 (lima) bermakna 5 (lima) sila Pancasila; **b. roda gigi
> bermakna Polinema merupakan sebuah lembaga pendidikan yang bergerak di bidang
> sains dan teknologi; c. bola dunia bermakna dunia dapat dikuasai melalui
> penguasaan sains dan teknologi; d. tugu Kota Malang bermakna lokasi
> keberadaan Polinema; e. 3 (tiga) lembar buku bermakna tridharma perguruan
> tinggi; f. warna biru dan hitam bermakna pendidikan yang berkualitas; g. warna
> oranye bermakna semangat dan kegigihan untuk belajar dan bersaing dalam

### 4c. Document ordering

Statuta 2014 (cited by the Atribut page) is **superseded** by Permenristekdikti
20/2019 (the 2019 Berita Negara entry is titled "Statuta. **Pencabutan**."

## 6. Documented brand rules

**There is no published, downloadable brand guideline / *pedoman identitas
visual* on any live Polinema domain.**

What *does* exist, ranked by authority:

1. **Permenristekdikti No. 20 Tahun 2019 (Statuta Polinema), Pasal 3–8** —
   the de facto brand standard. Mandates element shapes, colours (RGB), and the
   typeface for the lambang lettering (Arial). It does *not* cover clear-space,
   minimum size, lockup variants, digital usage, or a colour for anything
   outside the marks listed. → `docs/research/sources/statuta-polinema-permenristekdikti-20-2019.pdf`
2. **Archived *Atribut* page** — prose meanings + department flag colours +
   apparel rules. Subdomain dead; see §4b.
3. **Site-local CSS tokens** — `jti.polinema.ac.id` is the only Polinema host
   with a real design-token file (`--yellow-01…11`, `--neutral-01…11`,
   `--orange-1/2-01…11`, `--blue-01…11`, `--red-01…11`, gradients, type scale).
   It is engineering convention, **not** an institutional standard, and no
   other Polinema site shares it.

**Approved typefaces, to the extent they are documented at all:**
`Arial` (lambang lettering — Statuta, explicit); `Planet Kosmos` (legacy logo
wordmark — Statuta, explicit); `Open Sans` (polinema.ac.id CSS);
`Helvetica/Arial` (jti.polinema.ac.id CSS). There is **no** institutionally
approved typeface list.

## 8. UNVERIFIED / NOT FOUND

Stated explicitly, per instructions. Nothing below should be asserted as fact.

**A. Official SVG of the Polinema lambang or wordmark — DOES NOT EXIST publicly.**
Seven probes returned 404 (HTML 404 bodies, not SVG):
`themes/polinema/images/lambang.svg`, `themes/polinema/images/logo.svg`,
`themes/polinema/images/misc/logo.svg`,
`uploads/2023/02/lambang-polinema-blu.svg`,
`uploads/2025/05/lambang-polinema-blu-5_new-4.svg`,
`webjti-theme/.../logo-JTI.svg`, `webjti-theme/.../logo.svg`.
The only `.svg` on either site (`Logos Jurusan.svg`) contains **embedded raster
PNGs**, not vector paths. ⇒ Maximum available quality is 1140×1138 raster.

**B. `Satya Wira Prasetya` as Polinema's motto — NOT FOUND.**
Searched the Statuta full text (`pdftotext`); grepped for `satya|wira|prasetya|
semboyan|slogan|motto|moto` — zero hits. Brave search for
`"Satya Wira Prasetya" polinema` returned only unrelated persons, films, and an
Instagram account. The archived *Atribut* page lists a "Moto" TOC entry but its
body is missing from the snapshot. **Do not treat this phrase as Polinema's
motto.**

**C. No institutional motto or tagline of any kind is documented.** Not in the
Statuta; not on `/visi-misi/`, `/profil/`, `/sejarah/`; not in the homepage
footer or meta. The homepage has no `<meta name="description">` at all.

**D. No published brand guideline / *pedoman identitas visual* exists on a live
host.** Three such pages are indexed by search engines but all are unreachable:

| URL | Status 2026-09-27 |
|---|---|
| `https://devel-jti.polinema.ac.id/profil/panduan-identitas-visual/` | NXDOMAIN |
| `https://devel-jti.polinema.ac.id/home/konten-web/panduan-visual-identitas-logo-dan-lambang-polinema/` | NXDOMAIN |
| `https://devel-www.polinema.ac.id/en/profile/identity-guidelines/` | NXDOMAIN |
| `https://devel-an.polinema.ac.id/konten-web/panduan-visual-identitas-logo-dan-lambang-polinema/` | NXDOMAIN |
| `https://akuntansi.polinema.ac.id/profil/panduan-identitas-visual/` | resolves, but HTTP **503** on both `http` and `https`; cert subject is `ubuntu-nodejs` (unrelated host) |

None are in the Wayback Machine (`"Wayback Machine has not archived that URL"`
for all probed variants). The Internet Archive CDX API was offline throughout
("Internet Archive: Temporarily Offline"), so I could not exhaustively enumerate
snapshots — **this is a gap, not a proof of absence.** The pages may have
existed; I could not read them.

**E. Spec-vs-artwork colour divergence is unexplained.** Statuta shield blue
`#004E98` and gear orange `#EB5F10` match none of the four shipped rasters
(`#2D2766`/`#2D1B6B`, `#E87817`/`#E77817`). Either the artwork predates or
postdates the regulation, or the regulation transcribes an older drawing.
No source reconciles them. **Do not silently pick one** — flag it to whoever
owns the design decision.

**F. Wordmark colour is not stable across exports.** `#00095D`+`#FF6E40`
(2023 master) vs `#021265`+`#EF7805` (live 2025 header). No spec covers the
wordmark colours at all — the Statuta only regulates the lambang, the legacy
logo, flags, and apparel.

**G. The JTI monogram has no published spec.** Its colours are measured (§3f)
and align with `webjti-theme` tokens, but no document states when or how it may
be used. Treat it as an undocumented decorative asset. No JTI-specific RGB code
appears in the Statuta — the Statuta only regulates the Polinema lambang, the
legacy logo, flags, and apparel.

**H. The Peraturan Direktur that "further regulates" the lambang and logo**
(referenced by Statuta Pasal 3(5), 4(5), 5(3), 6(3), 8(6)) — **not located**.
If it exists and is public, it is the document that would settle items A, E, F
above and would be the closest thing to a real brand guideline.

**I. The pildir.polinema.ac.id mirror of the Statuta timed out** (>2 min,
twice). The BPK RI mirror
(`https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/131546/…`) downloaded cleanly and is
what I cite and saved.

**J. Out of scope, not checked:** HMTI's own brand assets, the `hmti.polinema.ac.id`
host if one exists, and any 2026-era Peraturan Direktur. A sibling thread owns
the HMTI-side recon.
